The Studio

For over two decades, Executech has renovated some of Manhattan's most demanding co-ops, condos, and townhouses — the kind of projects where tolerances are measured in millimeters and there is no room to improvise on site.

We're bringing that same discipline north, to the pre-war colonials, mid-century estates, and modern new-builds of Westchester County. Every project is treated as a singular piece of architecture — not a catalog of finishes — and managed by one team from first sketch to final walkthrough, so nothing gets lost in translation between designer, architect, and trade.

Process

Four stages. No surprises.

Working alongside your architect or designer — or ours — we develop drawings, materials, and a fixed budget before a single wall comes down.
